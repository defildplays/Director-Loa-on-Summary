03 / COMBAT & SUPPORTS

Class Balance Is Changing

The direction is smaller gaps in DPS, utility, difficulty-to-reward and support performance.

NOV 18 · KR
November 18 · new combat mechanic

Multi-Crit: Crit Chance Above 100% Will Matter

The new Multi-Crit system converts excess Crit Rate into a chance for a higher-damage critical hit, rather than wasting Crit Rate above 100%. The stream gave this illustration:

NON-CRIT100White number
NORMAL CRIT200Yellow number
MULTI-CRIT300Red number
At 120% total Crit Rate, every hit crits; the extra 20% becomes a Multi-Crit chance. The example uses 200% normal Crit Damage and 300% Multi-Crit damage on a 100-damage base hit. Real results depend on the final implementation and your character's stats.

Party Crit Synergy and class damage will be adjusted alongside this system. The team wants better parity across classes, not just a higher average DPS.

November 18 · Bard & Artist

Ground Attack Buffs Become Easier

Sonic Vibration (Bard) and Sun Well (Artist) will keep their Attack Power buff active for its full duration once a party member steps into the buff area. They should no longer need to stand inside it the entire time.

November 18 · Paladin & Valkyrie

Buff Skill Cooldowns Aligned

The two rotating Attack Power buff skills for Paladin and Valkyrie are planned to have matching cooldowns, making buff cycling easier to track.

November 18 · all supports

Brand Duration +50%

Brand debuff durations are being increased by 50% (to 150% of their current duration). Fewer refreshes should reduce the support's mechanical workload.

Winter · broader support overhaul

Lower Support Damage Dependency

Support contribution has reached roughly 44% in recent content according to the presentation, with some cases even higher. The development team plans to reduce how much a party's total damage depends on its support, while retaining support value and progression.

No exact formula or percentage reduction was announced.

Longer-term class direction

Less “Easy Class vs Hard Class” Disparity

Class balancing will account for DPS, stagger, counters, Push Immunity, utility and execution difficulty. Existing underperforming/dated classes are a priority. No new class is planned for summer 2027, reserving development time for existing class improvements.

04 / PROGRESSION

Part 1 → Part 2: What Happens To Your Gear?

A growth-system conversion, not an announced complete loss of character progress. The actual rates and mechanics are due in a later briefing.

The director specifically called out top-end bracelets as a costly system. The summary references roughly the top 20% of bracelet investment for high-end treatment, not a blanket 20% refund.

Gem overhaul

Roster-Wide Gems

Gems will become a roster-wide resource, moving away from today's character-by-character gem handling. Skill-based equipping and level expansion are planned. Do not assume a specific gold refund, conversion ratio or gem cap until the November 20 briefing.

Card system

Always-Active Card Sets

Collected Card Set effects should apply automatically through a catalogue-style system. This changes the need for swapping sets between content. Underused effects may get rebalance adjustments; spending on extra Card Preset slots is to be refunded.

05 / NEW AI COMPANION SYSTEM

Build Your Own NPC Party

A major expansion of Solo content that also introduces collectible, upgradeable NPC teammates and new modes.

WINTER · KR
Acquired through gameplay — not a gacha system

Arkesia Companions

Recruit well-known Lost Ark NPCs, including characters such as Nineveh. Assign them DPS or Support roles, choose a party setup and take them into compatible solo content. The developers want the companions to function more like real raid teammates than passive followers.

CountersNPCs react to boss counters
StaggerThey assist with Stagger checks
AwakeningManual or automatic use

Use cases discussed: Solo Raids, Chaos Rift, Ebony Cube and other supported solo encounters. Player-required group raids remain group raids. Kadan was not announced as a recruitable companion.

New battle mode

Guardian Frontier

Fight Guardian-style bosses with your Companions, including an enhanced Hanumatan variant provisionally called Agmatan. Competitive/leaderboard challenges are also being explored for stronger players.

Companion dispatch

Companion Expeditions

Send your NPC party to clear stages automatically. Higher cleared stages unlock additional rewards, including resources that improve Companions or progress your character.

06 / WINTER PREVIEW

New World, Dailies & Horizontal Content

These features were previewed for Lost Ark's next major direction. Individual launch dates have not all been confirmed.

WINTER / FUTURE
New class

Female Sharpshooter

The next class planned for Winter 2026 in KR is Female Sharpshooter (female version of the Sharpshooter class). Skill identity and exact launch date were not fully detailed in the livestream.

New sea region

Sea of Aldebaran

A new ocean region named after the God of Life. Plans include 17 islands and 4 Adventure Islands, new sea resources and story landmarks. Older Adventure Islands will be retired or adapted for solo play; AI participants are being considered for events such as Snowpang Island.

Story expansion

Sacria: Rasamoa

A new Sacrian continent/location, provisionally translated Rasamoa, advances the War of the Gods storyline and introduces new locations and encounters. Proper nouns await official English localization.

Daily content consolidation

Adventurer Requests

Existing daily systems — Chaos Dungeons, Guardian Raids, Chaos Rifts, Ebony Cubes, Una's Tasks and related content — are planned to feed into a shared Request system. Complete tasks for NPCs to build reputation, recruit them as Companions and level existing Companions.

Up to five regular completions per roster were discussed, with further character activity handled by faster completion. Exact limits and reward rules need confirmation.

Weekly rewards

Golden Compass

A weekly reward system intended to recognize the roster's activity. The presentation framed this as a reward to look forward to collecting at the Wednesday weekly reset.

Massive public encounter

World Bosses

Separate from the current Field Boss model: players across multiple channels fight a shared boss HP pool at the same time. Channels compete for damage contribution and special achievements such as a finishing blow, which can award bonus rewards.

Horizontal progression

Adventure Tome Overhaul

Adventure Tome completion becomes more overarching, with rewards assigned according to total completion across the continents rather than only isolated individual-continent progress.

New fishing activity

Open-Sea Fishing

Catch and record fish sizes. The largest recorded catch of a species can earn a world-record title, giving fishing a new competitive and collectible angle.

Social progression

Peyto Members

A new membership / reputation system connected to the Vedicce family, with ranks such as Guest, Friend and Partner. Build points and coins to unlock benefits and social content.

Minigames

Toypia

A colorful leisure hub unlocked through Peyto membership, featuring co-op and competitive minigames, including a concept described as Survival Chess.

07 / ENDGAME & DESIGN

New Raid Schedule & Difficulty Philosophy

A planned annual raid cadence and a move away from short, ultra-restrictive Extreme progression.

2027 ROADMAP
One raid approximately every three months

Four Raids a Year — Different Difficulty Levels

01 · Shadow RaidMain endgame pillar (Belgardin style)
02 · Mid RaidLighter / more accessible raid
03 · New Harder RaidNew challenging raid type planned for summer 2027
04 · Mid RaidSecond lighter raid

This is the intended recurring yearly model, not a published calendar with exact raid names and release dates.

Challenging content

Less Time-Limited Extreme Pressure

Director Lee said the recent Act 3 Mordum Extreme and Denouement Kazeros Extreme implementations missed the mark. They do not plan immediate nerfs, but future high-difficulty content should resemble a The First style challenge that remains attainable as players progress, rather than an overly compressed power check.

Raid accessibility

Newcomers Need A Better Solo → Party Path

Solo content has brought players in but not always prepared them for group raids. AI Companions and more gradual difficulty progression are intended to bridge that gap. Shorter gate counts should not lead to overloaded 20-minute fights with excessively punishing restarts.

08 / ECONOMY & PARADISE

Gold Economy, Paradise & Hell Keys

The developers addressed gold devaluation, content reward inflation and the impact of cash-shop packages.

NEXT PARADISE SEASON
Paradise Season changes

Paradise Hell Keys Reduced Outside Top 100

For ranks outside the top 100, Hell Key rewards will be reduced to 2 keys in the next season. This targets the impact Paradise rewards, especially Fusion Materials, are having on the wider economy.

The stream did not announce that every player only gets two keys.

Paradise Season changes

No More One-Time Crucible Farming

The system will be adjusted so players cannot complete Crucible only once per season and then keep collecting key rewards. Weekly participation will be required. Detailed mechanics are still pending.

Game economy

Why Gold Prices Have Fallen

The slides highlighted weaker demand from fewer economically active players, fast progression creating more gold supply, reduced gold consumption from existing progression systems, and lower reward value relative to player time.

Cash shop discussion

What The “2.9%” Figure Really Means

The previous 2.9% figure refers to a specific high-output week when a package launched, measured against gold creation. It does not mean cash-shop packages have a flat 2.9% impact on the entire economy.
